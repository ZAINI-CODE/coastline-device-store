# Proposed returns policy — NOT APPROVED / NOT PUBLISHED

Owner review required before use. This is a proposed 30-day policy beginning on the date the customer receives the order, not a legal guarantee. Review against applicable consumer protection requirements.

Customers would request a return within 30 days of delivery, providing the order reference and proof of purchase. The owner must approve eligibility, specify the required condition and packaging, and decide treatment of opened products, accessories, digital content, consumables, damage and misuse. Applicable rights for defective or misdescribed goods must be preserved.

Customers should contact the configured support channel before shipping a return. The owner must provide return authorization and an actual return address. Do not use the supplied boulevard-only location as a shipping address.

Owner decisions required: who pays return shipping for ordinary returns, how damaged or defective arrivals are handled, inspection criteria, any lawful exclusions, restocking fees if any, refund method, processing times and escalation instructions. No values have been invented for these decisions. PayPal refunds should be issued through the actual provider account and reconciled with order records; Zelle refunds require a separate bank-controlled process.
